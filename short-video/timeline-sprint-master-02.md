# Shorts/Reels Master 02 — Timeline Sprint

## Purpose
A premium 9:16 chronological storytelling template for INTRST Films short-form content. Built for fast, clear progression through 5–6 key moments in a subject's life, career, movement, or legacy.

## Best use cases
- Before They Were Icons
- Rise / fall / comeback arcs
- Historical figures and movements
- Career milestone stories
- Legacy-in-perspective stories

## Format
- 1080 × 1920 (9:16)
- 45–60 seconds
- 5–6 timeline beats maximum
- One main visual idea per beat

## Narrative timing
1. 0–2 sec — hook
2. 2–6 sec — early days / context
3. 6–14 sec — turning point
4. 14–24 sec — rise / impact
5. 24–36 sec — challenge / setback / cost
6. 36–50 sec — legacy / meaning
7. 50–60 sec — soft CTA

## Visual rules
- Editorial documentary feel, not social-template styling
- Real, verified imagery preferred
- Years and dates are primary visual anchors
- Use strong chronological progression
- One hero image per beat whenever possible
- Subject identity must pass the visual integrity gate before production
- Avoid collage clutter, tiny copy, excessive decorative elements, and flashy transitions

## Text rules
- 6–8 words maximum per screen for headline-level text
- Large, bold years/dates
- Supporting text should be short and subordinate
- Keep text away from faces and platform UI zones

## Motion
- Subtle push-in or slow zoom on stills
- Controlled parallax where useful
- Smooth timeline progression
- Match cuts or restrained slide transitions
- Avoid gimmicky TikTok transitions

## Audio
- Calm, authoritative narration
- Cinematic music bed that builds gradually
- Light ambient or archival texture where appropriate
- Beat-synced cuts can be used sparingly

## Brand treatment
- Minimal INTRST FILMS signature
- Midnight Navy / Deep Slate base tones
- Warm Ivory typography
- Dawn Gold used sparingly for years, rules, and accent markers
- No large logos

## CTA options
- Follow for more stories worth remembering.
- Watch the full story on INTRST Films.
- Save this story. Some lives deserve a second look.

## n8n inputs
- `subject_name`
- `story_angle`
- `hook_line`
- `timeline_beats[]`
  - `year`
  - `title`
  - `supporting_line`
  - `approved_asset_id`
- `key_takeaway`
- `voiceover_script`
- `cta_line`
- `music_style`
- `brand_mark_url`

## Production gate
Every timeline image must be verified for subject, era, event, and contextual accuracy. Do not use AI-generated likenesses of real subjects when authentic licensed, editorial, institutional, or public-domain imagery is available.
