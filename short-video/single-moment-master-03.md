# INTRST Films — Shorts/Reels Master 03 — Single Moment

## Purpose

A premium 9:16 vertical storytelling system built around one decisive event, scene, appearance, speech, decision, or symbolic moment. This is the most cinematic and emotionally focused short-form template in the initial INTRST Films vertical family.

## Best use cases

- One defining speech or public appearance
- One decisive career or life moment
- One final public moment when verified and editorially appropriate
- One symbolic historical event
- One turning point whose meaning matters more than chronology

## Structure

Recommended runtime: 45–60 seconds.

- 0:00–0:02 — Hook: the moment
- 0:02–0:06 — Set the scene
- 0:06–0:18 — What happened
- 0:18–0:30 — Why it mattered
- 0:30–0:45 — The impact
- 0:45–0:55 — Takeaway / meaning
- 0:55–1:00 — Soft CTA

## Visual doctrine

- One dominant moment per story.
- Prefer authentic, licensed, editorial, institutional, archival, or public-domain imagery.
- Keep image selection traceable and identity-verified before production.
- Use slow push-ins, restrained parallax, subtle film texture, deliberate holds, and smooth cinematic transitions.
- Avoid fast montage logic, flashy creator transitions, over-animation, clutter, AI-looking portrait generation, and generic motivational aesthetics.
- Text should support the moment, never cover the face or critical documentary evidence.
- Keep branding subtle; the story must feel editorial, not promotional.

## Text rules

- 6–10 words maximum per screen whenever possible.
- One idea per beat.
- Headlines should describe tension, consequence, or meaning rather than summarize the whole story.
- Supporting copy stays concise and factual.
- No sensationalized claims.

## Asset requirements

- Verified image of the exact moment whenever possible
- Supporting context image or B-roll if required
- Verified subject portrait
- Location/environment footage or stills when relevant
- Final voiceover
- Cinematic/subtle music bed
- Captions
- Minimal INTRST Films brand mark

## Identity and authenticity gate

Every real-person asset must pass the INTRST identity workflow before rendering:

1. trusted source retrieval
2. source metadata/context validation
3. vision/reference match where needed
4. license/usage status recorded
5. crop verification
6. final approval status

AI-generated imagery is not the default for documentary identity or historical evidence. It may be used only for clearly conceptual, symbolic, environmental, or transitional visuals when appropriate and not presented as authentic archival material.

## Selection logic

Choose this master when:

- the story is strongest around one specific event rather than a full life arc;
- one verified visual can carry substantial emotional or historical meaning;
- the editorial objective is reflection, consequence, or context;
- slower premium pacing is preferable to rapid chronological progression.

Do not use when the story requires 4–6 milestones to make sense; use Timeline Sprint instead. Do not use when the primary objective is a fast curiosity-driven subject reveal; use Hook Reveal instead.

## Premium standard

The finished short should feel like a compressed documentary scene, not a social media template. Pacing, crop, typography, voiceover, music, and image authenticity must all reinforce that standard.
