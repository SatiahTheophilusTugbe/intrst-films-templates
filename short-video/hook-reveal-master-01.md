# INTRST Films — Shorts/Reels Master 01: Hook Reveal

## Purpose
Primary premium vertical storytelling master for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok, and Facebook Reels.

## Format
- Canvas: 1080 × 1920 (9:16)
- Duration target: 35–60 seconds
- Designed for subject-led, emotionally focused stories

## Best use cases
- defining moments
- newly deceased / recent legacy stories
- what-most-people-missed angles
- one decision that changed everything
- emotionally resonant subject reveals

## Narrative timing
1. 0–2s — unresolved hook
2. 2–6s — subject reveal
3. 6–18s — context
4. 18–35s — escalation / emotional turn
5. 35–50s — payoff
6. 50–60s — reflective CTA

## Visual grammar
- one dominant verified subject
- premium portrait or authentic archival/editorial image
- strong but plausible directional light
- cinematic negative space
- Midnight Navy / Deep Slate base
- Warm Ivory primary type
- Dawn Gold micro-accents only
- subtle INTRST FILMS signature
- restrained lower-third system

## Text rules
- hook: 2–6 words preferred
- max 6–10 words visible on screen at once
- no paragraph blocks over moving footage
- captions must stay inside mobile-safe regions
- hierarchy: hook > subject/context > CTA

## Motion rules
- slow push-ins
- subtle reframing
- layered parallax where appropriate
- clean archival transitions
- no flashy social-template transitions
- no aggressive zoom-punches, emoji, arrows, stickers, or gimmick overlays

## Authenticity rules
- real subjects require verified imagery through the Subject Identity Verification layer
- authentic, licensed, editorial, institutional, or public-domain photography is preferred
- AI-generated likenesses should not represent real people when authentic imagery exists
- final crops must pass subject/crop verification

## Automation inputs
- subject_id
- subject_name
- hook_line
- story_angle
- key_beats
- approved_asset_ids
- voiceover_script
- subtitle_segments
- lower_third
- location_or_year
- cta_line
- source_credits
- identity_confidence
- crop_verified

## CTA examples
- Full story on INTRST Films.
- Follow for stories worth remembering.
- Save this story. Some lives deserve a second look.

## Premium constraint
The finished short must feel editorial and human-directed. Automation may assemble the piece, but it must not look like a generic AI video, quote reel, slideshow, or prebuilt social-media template.
