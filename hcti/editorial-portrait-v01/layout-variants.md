# INTRST Editorial Portrait — Controlled Layout Variants

Status: WORKING / REVIEW ONLY

All variants inherit the same locked working grammar: full-bleed photography, glossy contemporary image treatment, Signal Blue identifier system, Dawn Gold tagline, Warm Ivory narrative typography, source metadata, and CTA hidden by default.

## `variant-left` — Editorial Portrait
Default for portraits with usable negative space on the left.

- subject weighted right or center-right
- headline lower-left
- strongest match to the approved Dolly visual direction
- use when face readability and editorial negative space can coexist

## `variant-bottom` — Feature Opener
For portraits that need more horizontal freedom or cannot support a large left text column.

- subject may sit closer to center
- stronger bottom scrim
- headline spans more of the lower field
- useful for tighter portraits and landscape-derived crops

## `variant-offset` — Contemporary Journal
Selective higher-tension treatment.

- headline begins farther into the composition
- more overlap between typography and photographic field
- lower left remains available for source/provenance
- use sparingly; intended as the controlled adventurous variant rather than default

## Selection rule
Choose layout from asset geometry and safe text space, not randomly.

Priority order:
1. `variant-left`
2. `variant-bottom`
3. `variant-offset`

Do not create additional variants until these three fail a real subject requirement or cross-subject testing demonstrates a repeatable need.
