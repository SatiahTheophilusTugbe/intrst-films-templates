# INTRST Films — Episode 001
## Hayden Panettiere — Master Edit Blueprint v1

Target runtime: 12–15 minutes. Narrator: INTRST Narrator 01.

## Directorial intent
Premium documentary essay. Real imagery establishes reality; narration establishes meaning; design establishes brand; AI remains mostly invisible. No fake archival footage, no AI likeness presented as evidence, no tabloid montage, no ambulance spectacle.

## Visual grammar
- 16:9, 4K preferred.
- Midnight Navy/Cinematic Black base; Warm Ivory type; Dawn Gold micro-accents.
- Authentic stills: subtle 3–6% push or restrained parallax only.
- Copyrighted film/TV excerpts: generally 2–6 seconds, commentary-driven, logged in clip ledger, original score muted/reduced unless analytically necessary.
- Interviews: prioritize Hayden speaking in her own words, especially 2026 memoir press.
- Death section remains minimal, factual and approximately 10–15% of runtime.
