# INTRST Films — Episode 001 Editorial Brief

## Subject
Hayden Panettiere

## Working Title
**She Grew Up in Front of Us**

## Secondary Title
**A Life Lived in Public**

## Core Thesis
Hayden Panettiere spent almost her entire life being watched. She entered the business before she could form memories, became famous while still growing up, and spent adulthood increasingly speaking in her own voice about the person behind the public image.

## Narrative Contradiction
The public knew her face for decades, but much of her later life was about defining herself beyond roles, expectations and scrutiny that arrived before adulthood.

## Emotional Question
**What happens when the world forms an idea of you before you have had the chance to form one of yourself?**

## Approved Arc
1. **Before Memory** — commercials at 11 months; childhood soaps.
2. **The World Learns Her Name** — major film and television roles; Heroes and Nashville.
3. **The Cost of Visibility** — only publicly documented struggles; no amateur diagnosis or causal claims.
4. **Grief and Return** — brother Jansen's death; return as Kirby Reed in Scream VI.
5. **Taking the Story Back** — memoir; public discussion of identity, privacy and how others perceived her.
6. **The Final Chapter** — verified death facts only; cause remains officially undetermined as of 2026-08-22.
7. **What Remains** — close on authorship and legacy, not tragedy.

## Editorial Guardrails
- Do not call overdose the confirmed cause of death unless an official authority later confirms it.
- Do not imply her history of addiction caused her death.
- Do not reduce the subject to addiction, relationships, scandal or a 'troubled star' trope.
- Distinguish factual claims from interpretation.
- Every factual script line must map to the approved Claim Ledger.
- Keep the final chapter to roughly 10–15% of long-form runtime unless verified developments materially change the story.

## Visual Motif
Cameras and flashes versus increasingly quiet private-feeling frames: childhood publicity, stages and press lines gradually giving way to still portraits, books, dressing-room emptiness and negative space.

## Long-Form Target
12–15 minute documentary essay. Authentic archival evidence establishes reality; narration establishes meaning; design establishes brand; AI remains largely invisible.
