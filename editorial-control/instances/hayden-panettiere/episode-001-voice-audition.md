# INTRST Films — Episode 001 Voice Audition

## Subject
Hayden Panettiere

## Purpose
Test candidate ElevenLabs voices against the full emotional range required by INTRST: factual authority, reflective intimacy, forward momentum, sensitive material, and legacy. Render the exact same passage with each candidate voice and score using `longform-video/narration-direction-v1.md`.

## Target runtime
Approximately 75–95 seconds depending on voice and pacing.

## Performance direction
- Overall pace: 142–150 WPM.
- Start at emotional temperature 2.
- Rise briefly to 3 during career recognition.
- Move to 4 with restraint during the identity/pressure section.
- End at 2–3: reflective, unresolved, not mournful.
- No trailer voice.
- Do not perform the final lines as a eulogy.

## Audition copy

Before most people form their first memories, Hayden Panettiere was already working. //

At eleven months old, she appeared in a commercial. Childhood brought soap operas, film roles, and eventually the kind of recognition that changes how the world sees you. By seventeen, millions knew her as Claire Bennet in *Heroes*. Later, *Nashville* showed another side of her talent — and earned her two Golden Globe nominations. //

But there is another way to read that career. Hayden did not simply become famous. / She grew up inside fame. //

For much of her life, the public watched the roles, the relationships, the successes, and the struggles. In adulthood, she spoke openly about addiction, postpartum depression, grief, and the pressure of living so much of a private life in public. ///

In 2026, she published a memoir in her own voice. It arrived at a moment when her story seemed to be moving again — not backward toward the child star people remembered, but forward toward a woman deciding how she wanted to be understood. //

Hayden Panettiere died at thirty-six. Her official cause of death remains undetermined. [hold]

The headline tells us how the story ended. //
The life asks a different question: what happens when the world learns your name before you have fully learned yourself?

## Audition scorecard
Score each voice 1–10, then convert against the weighted rubric in Narration Direction v1.

- Naturalness
- Documentary authority
- Emotional restraint
- Warmth
- Long-form listenability
- Pronunciation
- Distinctiveness

## Required test notes
For each candidate record:
- voice name / voice ID
- model
- stability / similarity / style settings if applicable
- speed setting
- render date
- specific pronunciation problems
- synthetic cadence moments
- strongest passage
- weakest passage
- total score
- approve / reject / backup

## Approval rule
Do not select a voice because it sounds the most dramatic. Select the voice that makes the writing feel most credible and human over a 15-minute listen.
