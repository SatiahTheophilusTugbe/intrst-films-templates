# INTRST Films — Story Bible v1

## Purpose
The Story Bible is the editorial control layer that turns verified facts into a coherent, premium narrative without allowing unsupported invention. It defines what the story is really about before any carousel, single-image post, short, or long-form script is written.

## Core Principle
Facts determine what we can say. The Story Bible determines what the story means.

## Required Fields

### 1. Subject
- `subject_id`
- `subject_name`
- `story_status` — breaking, recent, evergreen, historical, living

### 2. Trigger
What made this story timely now?

### 3. Verified Facts Summary
A concise summary of only approved Claim Ledger facts.

### 4. The Unknown
What remains unresolved, disputed, unconfirmed, or intentionally omitted?

### 5. Core Thesis
One sentence explaining what the story is really about.

A strong thesis should be:
- human, not encyclopedic
- specific to this subject
- supported by approved claims
- broad enough to power multiple formats

### 6. Narrative Contradiction
The tension that makes the life compelling.

Examples of structure:
- success vs private cost
- public identity vs private self
- power vs vulnerability
- achievement vs sacrifice
- fame vs anonymity
- survival vs time

### 7. Emotional Question
What should the audience wonder or feel as the story unfolds?

### 8. Audience Promise
What will the viewer understand by the end that the headline alone does not tell them?

### 9. Approved Interpretations
List each editorial interpretation and the Claim IDs supporting it.

### 10. Prohibited Angles
Explicitly identify sensational, unsupported, reductive, repetitive, or legally risky framings that must not appear.

### 11. Narrative Arc
Recommended chapter logic:
1. contradiction / cold open
2. origin
3. first break
4. rise
5. peak
6. cost / hidden tension
7. turning point or reclaiming the story
8. final chapter
9. legacy / what remains

Not every story needs all nine beats; the sequence should be adapted to the subject.

### 12. Visual Motif
A recurring visual idea that represents the story.

Examples:
- cameras and flashes
- handwritten pages
- empty stage
- factory floor
- childhood street
- scoreboard
- architectural structure

### 13. Evidence Motifs
Which real artifacts can carry authenticity?
- archival photos
- documents
- interviews
- headlines
- letters
- awards
- locations
- objects
- film/performance clips
- public records

### 14. Pacing Plan
Define where the story should feel:
- impact
- context
- acceleration
- quiet
- tension
- reflection
- resolution

### 15. Final Thought
What should remain in the audience’s mind five minutes after consuming the story?

### 16. Format Adaptation Notes
Define how the same thesis changes across:
- carousel
- single image
- Threads
- X
- LinkedIn
- TikTok photo carousel
- short-form video
- long-form documentary

## Quality Gate
A Story Bible is approved only if:
- the thesis is not merely a chronology,
- the contradiction is specific and defensible,
- all interpretations map to Claim IDs,
- unresolved facts remain unresolved,
- the angle does not reduce the subject to death, scandal, trauma, or achievement alone,
- the story has enough visual evidence to sustain premium output,
- the final thought is distinct from the opening headline.

## Editorial Rule
The Story Bible is the only approved narrative brief supplied to downstream writing models. Models may vary wording, rhythm, and platform-native structure, but may not change the thesis, invent new factual claims, or introduce prohibited angles without returning to editorial review.
