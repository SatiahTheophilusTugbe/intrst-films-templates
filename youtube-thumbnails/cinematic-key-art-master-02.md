# YouTube Thumbnail Master 02 — Cinematic Key Art

## Purpose
Premium 16:9 YouTube documentary key art for INTRST Films. This is the higher-tension thumbnail direction and should be used when the episode has a strong emotional contradiction, defining moment, comeback, cost-of-greatness angle, or high-stakes human story.

## Core composition
- One verified subject only.
- Subject face / upper body dominates roughly 55–65% of the frame.
- Subject placed off-center with strong negative space opposite.
- 2–3 word emotional tension hook only.
- No subhead or body copy.
- Tiny INTRST FILMS signature only.

## Visual standard
- Netflix/A24 documentary key-art feel compressed for YouTube readability.
- Midnight Navy #0B1723 and Deep Slate #182938 as primary field.
- Warm Ivory #F1EEE6 for headline.
- Dawn Gold #C79A58 only as a micro-accent.
- Crisp eyes, believable skin texture, realistic hair, plausible directional light.
- Premium, human, editorial, restrained.

## Prohibited
- Arrows, circles, emoji, reaction-face exaggeration.
- Giant outlined text.
- Tabloid red.
- Split-screen collage.
- Decorative panels or infographic structures.
- Over-grading, fake film scratches, heavy haze, excessive grain.
- AI-looking faces or synthetic identity images when authentic imagery exists.

## Asset integrity
All real-person imagery must pass the Subject Identity Verification layer before production. Prefer authentic, licensed, editorial, official, archival, or public-domain imagery. AI-generated likenesses are not the default for subject identity.

## Selection logic
Use this template when:
- the episode has a strong human tension or contradiction;
- the subject has a compelling portrait with clear emotional read;
- CTR requires stronger visual impact than the quieter Face + Tension variant;
- one short hook can carry the concept.

Use the quieter Face + Tension variant when:
- the subject image itself is iconic enough;
- the episode is reflective, memorial, legacy-led, or less confrontational;
- a more restrained editorial tone is preferable.

## Hook rules
- 2–3 words preferred.
- Never duplicate the video title verbatim unless strategically necessary.
- Hook should create unresolved tension, not summarize the biography.
- Examples: `THE FINAL YEARS`, `WHAT FAME COST`, `BEFORE THE LEGEND`, `WHAT REMAINED`, `THE COMEBACK`.

## Human review gate
Before publishing, verify:
1. correct subject;
2. crop preserves face and expression;
3. hook reads at mobile size;
4. thumbnail remains premium and non-sensational;
5. image usage rights are recorded;
6. no obvious synthetic/AI artifacts.
