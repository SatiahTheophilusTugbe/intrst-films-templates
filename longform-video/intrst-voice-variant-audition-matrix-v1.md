# INTRST Films — Voice Variant Audition Matrix v1

Generate three proprietary variants from the same master identity. Use the exact same Hayden audition passage for all three.

## Variant A — The Editorial Filmmaker
**Bias:** balanced baseline; likely primary candidate.
- warm but restrained
- intelligent and observant
- medium-low register
- natural conversational cadence
- modest texture
- calm authority
- emotional restraint prioritized over drama

**Prompt adjustment:** Emphasize natural humanity, quiet intelligence, documentary intimacy, and controlled warmth. Keep pace fluid and sentence endings deliberate.

## Variant B — The Cinematic Humanist
**Bias:** slightly warmer and more emotionally resonant.
- same core age/accent/register
- more warmth in origin and legacy passages
- slightly softer attack on consonants
- greater intimacy without breathiness
- still restrained in death/struggle material

**Prompt adjustment:** Add subtle human warmth, empathy, and reflective depth while explicitly avoiding sentimentality, sadness performance, or inspirational cadence.

## Variant C — The Investigative Essayist
**Bias:** slightly sharper, more authoritative and analytical.
- same core age/accent/register
- crisper articulation
- slightly drier tone
- stronger intellectual authority
- less warmth than B, more forward presence
- still non-news-anchor and non-radio

**Prompt adjustment:** Emphasize clarity, intelligence, precision, and measured authority while keeping the delivery conversational and cinematic rather than journalistic.

# Scoring Rubric — 100 Points

| Category | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Naturalness / absence of AI cadence | 20 |
| Trust / credibility | 15 |
| Emotional restraint | 15 |
| Distinctive brand character | 15 |
| Long-form listenability | 15 |
| Warmth / humanity | 10 |
| Articulation / intelligibility | 5 |
| Range across story chapters | 5 |

## Hard Fail Conditions
A candidate is rejected regardless of total score if it:
- sounds like a movie trailer
- sounds like generic true crime
- performs sadness during death material
- exhibits repeated synthetic cadence/artifacts
- becomes tiring after 60–90 seconds
- sounds indistinguishable from common stock AI narrators

## Decision Rules
- 85+ and no hard fail: eligible
- 90+: preferred primary narrator territory
- 80–84: revise and regenerate
- below 80: reject

## Test Procedure
1. Generate A, B, C with the same Hayden audition passage.
2. Export dry audio files.
3. Name files:
   - `INTRST_Voice_A_001.wav`
   - `INTRST_Voice_B_001.wav`
   - `INTRST_Voice_C_001.wav`
4. Listen once without notes for instinctive reaction.
5. Listen again and score rubric.
6. Test top two under light INTRST music at dialogue-safe level.
7. Select primary + backup.
8. Save exact Voice ID/settings and record them in production configuration.

## Important
Do not choose the deepest or most dramatic voice by default. The winner is the voice that feels most human, trustworthy, distinctive, and sustainable across hundreds of stories.
