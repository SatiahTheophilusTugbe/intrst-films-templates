# ElevenLabs Voice Design Prompt — INTRST Films v1

Use this as the baseline Voice Design description. Generate three previews from this same core identity before changing the specification.

## Master Prompt
Male documentary narrator, perceived age late 30s to mid-40s, neutral American English accent with a subtle transatlantic polish. Medium-low register, warm and grounded, with slight natural texture and a lived-in human quality. Calm authority without sounding like a news anchor, radio host, movie trailer narrator, or motivational speaker. Intelligent, observant, intimate, and emotionally restrained. Natural conversational cadence with varied sentence rhythm and deliberate sentence endings. Capable of quiet intimacy during personal material, subtle forward momentum during career-rise passages, and spacious reflection during legacy passages. Never theatrical, ominous, breathy, sentimental, glossy, or over-polished. Avoid exaggerated bass, sing-song phrasing, excessive vocal fry, artificial sadness, or obvious synthetic cadence. The performance should feel like an accomplished documentary filmmaker telling a remarkable human story across a table. Premium, cinematic, trustworthy, human.

Version 2 — slightly warmer and more human

Use: Slight natural texture and imperfection in the voice. Warmth increases during reflective passages. Avoid perfectly even sentence cadence. Allow subtle conversational shifts in pace, small natural hesitations, and gently softened sentence endings. The speaker should sound like an intelligent documentary filmmaker speaking to one person, not a professional announcer reading copy. Never theatrical or overly grave.

Version 3 — slightly more editorial and textured
Use: Calm, observant documentary narrator with a naturally low male register. Intelligent and quietly authoritative, with subtle vocal texture and restrained emotional range. Slightly dry, thoughtful delivery with natural variation between sentences. Avoid announcer cadence, repeated melodic patterns, dramatic trailer delivery, and overly polished text-to-speech rhythm. Some thoughts should feel intimate and almost conversational; others can carry firmer editorial weight.


## Audition Goal
The winning design should remain convincing through:
1. factual biography
2. fast career momentum
3. emotionally difficult material
4. sensitive death reporting
5. reflective legacy writing

## Generation Discipline
Do not change the audition script between variants. Change only the voice-design emphasis so the comparison is controlled.
