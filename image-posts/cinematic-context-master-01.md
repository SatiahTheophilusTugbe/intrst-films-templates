# Cinematic Context Master 01

## Purpose
Premium 4:5 editorial social post and carousel-cover template for INTRST Films when the environment, architecture, location, or archival context is essential to the story.

## Positioning
This is not a thumbnail. It is a premium social editorial composition designed to feel art-directed, cinematic, and publication-grade while remaining crisp and legible on mobile.

## Visual grammar
- 4:5 portrait composition
- One primary subject integrated into a meaningful environment
- Strong environmental geometry or contextual architecture may frame the subject
- Brighter premium editorial treatment; avoid muddy blacks, heavy haze, and underexposure
- Photography carries the design
- Generous negative space around headline and subject
- Warm Ivory and natural skin tones against restrained navy/blue context
- Dawn Gold only as a micro-accent
- No decorative clutter, arrows, circles, emoji, loud gradients, or tabloid devices

## Typography
- Main hook: Cormorant Garamond SemiBold, usually 2–4 words
- Supporting line: Inter, concise and optional
- Micro-branding: Manrope, uppercase, restrained
- Brand label must remain clearly readable at mobile size without competing with the headline

## Locked palette
- Midnight Navy: #0B1723
- Deep Slate: #182938
- Horizon Blue: #375A72
- Warm Ivory: #F1EEE6
- Dawn Gold: #C79A58
- Cinematic Black: #080A0C

## Best uses
- Before They Were Icons
- The Cost of Greatness
- One Life, One Moment
- Lives That Changed Everything
- Stories where place, scale, career environment, or cultural context strengthens the narrative

## Content rules
- Hook should create tension or curiosity, not summarize the entire story
- Supporting copy should be one short thought only
- One dominant subject; avoid collages
- Environment must add story meaning, not serve as generic decoration
- Prefer real/licensed/editorial/archive photography where appropriate; AI scenes must never be presented as archival fact

## Automation fields
Recommended dynamic fields for future rendering:
- `subject_image`
- `context_image_or_environment`
- `headline`
- `supporting_line`
- `brand_label`
- `accent_mode`
- `subject_position`
- `text_zone`
- `source_credit_optional`

## Selection criteria
Use this template when:
1. strong contextual/environmental imagery exists,
2. the story benefits from visual scale or place,
3. the subject can remain clearly dominant,
4. the headline can fit cleanly in the available negative space.

Do not use when the available background is visually noisy or when a tight portrait provides a stronger emotional hook; use Editorial Portrait Master 01 instead.
