# Editorial Portrait Master 01

## Purpose
Premium 4:5 editorial social graphic for INTRST Films. Use for single-image posts and carousel covers.

## Canva Master
Design ID: `DAHTBuodkUQ`

## Format
- 4:5 portrait social post
- Primary use: Instagram / Facebook editorial image post
- Secondary use: carousel cover
- Not intended for YouTube thumbnails

## Creative Direction
- Premium documentary-magazine aesthetic
- Bright, crisp subject separation
- Photography carries the composition
- Controlled navy/dark slate background areas with warm ivory typography
- Dawn Gold used only as a micro-accent
- No haze, muddy blacks, heavy vignette, arrows, circles, emoji, loud gradients, or generic social-template styling

## Typography
- Display headline: Cormorant Garamond SemiBold
- Brand label: Manrope
- Supporting text: Inter only when necessary

## Composition Rules
- One dominant subject
- Strong facial readability
- Generous negative space
- Main hook: 2–4 words
- Brand label must remain clearly readable but subordinate to the story hook
- Keep decorative linework minimal and architectural
- Preserve visual breathing room around subject and headline

## Brand Palette
- Midnight Navy `#0B1723`
- Deep Slate `#182938`
- Horizon Blue `#375A72`
- Warm Ivory `#F1EEE6`
- Dawn Gold `#C79A58`
- Cinematic Black `#080A0C`

## Automation Inputs
Recommended dynamic fields for future automated versions:
- `subject_image`
- `hook_line_1`
- `hook_line_2`
- `brand_label`
- `accent_enabled`

## Selection Logic
Use when:
- the subject portrait is strong enough to carry the post
- the story angle is identity-, legacy-, achievement-, or defining-moment-led
- a clean premium editorial treatment is preferable to archival or object-led storytelling

Avoid when:
- there is no high-quality portrait
- the story depends heavily on archival context
- multiple people are required to understand the hook
