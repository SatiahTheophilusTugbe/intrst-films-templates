# INTRST Films — Evidence Spread Master v1

Status: APPROVED FORMAT DOCTRINE / implementation template WORKING until rendered QA.

## Purpose
Show how a source, document, image, quote, timeline, or record supports a claim and changes the interpretation of a story. Evidence is a reasoning format: claim → proof → meaning.

## Core eye path
1. See the source.
2. Notice the relevant detail.
3. Understand the implication.

## Core structure
1. Claim line — what is being established.
2. Primary evidence zone — source/document/photo/excerpt.
3. Detail/callout zone — crop, underline, inset, quote, date, or key datum.
4. Interpretive text zone — concise explanation of significance.
5. Provenance zone — source/date/origin.

## Hierarchy modes
### Source-first
Use when the artifact is visually strong or immediately understandable.

### Claim-first
Use when interpretation must lead the eye before the source makes sense.

### Detail-first
Use when one small source detail is the story.

## Visual behavior
- Use thin rules, underlines, crop insets, translucent highlight blocks, or numbered cues only when they clarify the evidence path.
- No red circles, arrows, breaking-news callouts, sensational markup, or fake annotations.
- Keep editorial annotation visually distinct from source material.
- Maintain enough source context to prevent misleading extraction.

## Copy budgets
- Claim/headline: 5–14 words.
- Interpretation: 15–40 words.
- Provenance: short and standardized.

## Retention logic
Evidence should contain a compact narrative arc:
- Claim: what question or assertion is on the table?
- Proof: what source materially supports it?
- Meaning: what changes once the proof is understood?

Mini-hooks may be created by withholding the implication until the highlighted detail is seen, but never by hiding necessary context or overstating certainty.

## Color behavior
- Signal Blue: precision, identity, active evidence marker.
- Dawn Gold: rare interpretive or emotional pivot.
- Warm Ivory: primary text.
- Source material should retain authentic color unless a non-destructive contrast treatment is clearly editorial and does not imply altered evidence.

## CTA rule
No CTA/handle by default.

## QA
Reject if:
- claim exceeds what the cited evidence supports;
- annotation obscures or alters source meaning;
- source/provenance is unclear;
- graphic treatment becomes tabloid or forensic-theater styling;
- the reader cannot follow source → detail → implication in one glance sequence.
