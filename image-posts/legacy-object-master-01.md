# Legacy Object Master 01

## Role
Premium 4:5 social editorial template for INTRST Films. Use when a meaningful object, artifact, archive item, photograph, document, place detail, or symbolic possession carries the story better than a portrait.

## Creative intent
This is an object-led documentary composition, not a generic vintage collage. The asset must feel photographed, tactile, specific, and human. The design should evoke a premium editorial feature or documentary campaign rather than AI-generated content.

## Visual grammar
- Primary object or artifact is the hero.
- Supporting objects may appear only when they materially deepen context.
- Use realistic lighting, texture, wear, dust, paper grain, scratches, handwriting, or environmental detail where appropriate.
- Avoid synthetic perfection, impossible prop combinations, floating objects, excessive glow, over-sharpening, fake bokeh, and obvious AI artifacts.
- Use INTRST Films palette and restrained editorial typography.
- Gold is a micro-accent, not a dominant luxury effect.
- Preserve generous negative space and clear hierarchy.
- Avoid decorative icon rows unless editorially justified.
- Avoid generic film-production props unless the subject actually relates to them.

## Copy hierarchy
1. INTRST FILMS micro-branding
2. 2–6 word editorial hook
3. Optional one- to three-line context statement
4. Optional short emphasis line
5. Small brand signature / tagline only when it does not compete with the story

## Approved master example
Hook: `Every Object Holds a Story.`
Supporting copy: `We uncover the moments, the people, and the truths behind the details.`

The example is a visual-system reference only. Production content must be rewritten for the actual subject and must not mechanically reuse this copy.

## Best-fit story angles
- What They Left Behind
- Final Chapters
- Forgotten Giants
- One Life, One Moment
- estate / archive / personal artifact stories
- an object tied to a defining decision or cultural moment

## Asset policy
Prefer, in order:
1. licensed or public-domain authentic photography of the actual object
2. editorial/source photography with appropriate rights
3. carefully art-directed symbolic photography
4. AI-generated imagery only where no authentic asset is available and where the image is not presented as documentary evidence

For real people and historical events, never fabricate an artifact and imply that it is genuine.

## Automation inputs
- `subject_name`
- `story_angle`
- `headline`
- `context_line`
- `emphasis_line`
- `hero_asset`
- `asset_type`
- `asset_authenticity` (`actual`, `symbolic`, `generated`)
- `asset_source`
- `rights_status`
- `brand_label`
- `tagline_optional`

## Selection rule
Choose this layout when the object itself creates curiosity or emotional meaning at thumbnail/feed size. Do not choose it simply because a portrait is unavailable.

## Premium quality gate
Reject an output if any of the following is true:
- it looks like an AI mood board
- there are too many unrelated props
- the image feels synthetic or over-rendered
- text density makes it feel like an ad
- visual effects overpower the story
- the asset could falsely be interpreted as authentic documentary evidence
- it deviates from the established INTRST editorial family without a clear story reason
